How to read this atlas

Dates order the stories; colors identify research threads. Parallel paths show work from different threads in the same historical period.

Connections show related questions and evidence, not a claim that every researcher directly influenced the next.

Dates can be approximate. Original illustrations, later portraits and modern diagrams are identified in their captions.
