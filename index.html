About the project

Research paths meet and branch.

Large cards record questions and evidence; the label inside each card names its research topic. Solid colored connections follow related experiments and show a three-way fork before Galvani. Dotted connections show later concepts brought together, without claiming direct influence. Smaller pink nodes trace names such as nervus, soma, dendrite, axon and synapse. Left to right is approximate historical order, not a proportional time scale.

Ancient evidence is incomplete, and several “first discovery” claims are disputed. The wording preserves those cautions instead of forcing a hero-only story.
