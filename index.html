About the project

Not a family tree - an evidence tree.

Each branch records a question, method, observation, model shift, limitation, and the next question it created. The historical claims and references come from the two supplied source PDFs.

Ancient evidence is incomplete, and several “first discovery” claims are disputed. The wording preserves those cautions instead of forcing a hero-only story.
