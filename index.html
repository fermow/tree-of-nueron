ABOUT THE ATLAS

One history. Many questions.

The six colored research threads are inquiries that often unfolded at the same time. Solid paths connect related evidence; dotted bridges show ideas that later met without implying every scientist directly influenced another. Small pink nodes follow the language of neuroscience. Horizontal spacing is for readable navigation, not a proportional date scale.

Each card opens a sourced explanation of the question, method, observation and limits of a discovery. Ancient evidence and several priority claims remain uncertain.
